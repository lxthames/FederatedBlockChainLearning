# Journal Manuscript Outline

## Working title

Stress-Testing Blockchain-Assisted Federated Learning Under Data Heterogeneity, Identity Errors, and Committee Capture

## Structure

1. Introduction: problem, evaluation gap, research questions, and contributions.
2. Related work: robust FL, non-IID Byzantine defenses, blockchain-assisted FL, accountability, and recovery.
3. Threat model and trust assumptions.
4. Benchmark methodology and executable contracts.
5. Corrected two-layer reference framework.
6. Experimental protocol, datasets, attacks, baselines, and metrics.
7. Core robustness and utility results.
8. Scale, heterogeneity, attacker-fraction, identity, and committee sensitivity.
9. Recovery, wrongful slashing, communication, runtime, and ledger overhead.
10. Failure boundaries, limitations, and external validity.
11. Conclusion restricted to demonstrated findings.

Appendices contain the claim-to-evidence matrix, complete configurations, baseline assumptions, additional seed-level plots, and complexity analysis. The submitted manuscript must be audited against this structure before text is reused.
